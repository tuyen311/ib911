🎯 Mission

Group activity · about 30 minutes · one phone or laptop per group

FICTIONAL CASE

NovaBrew is an imaginary Vietnamese specialty-coffee chain opening its first overseas hub in Tokyo. Your group is NovaBrew’s HR team. Make four decisions with evidence from Session 10, then present your plan in 2 minutes.

How to play (read first)

  1. Choose your group number and team name above.
  2. Do the 4 rounds in order. Every answer gives instant feedback; warnings are hints, not penalties.
  3. Your bar on the 🏆 Leaderboard moves as you finish rounds, so the whole class sees who is ahead.
  4. At the end paste your slide link + 1–2 sentences of reasoning, then press Submit.
